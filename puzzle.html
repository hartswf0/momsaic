The loop

How to play

  1. You have 24 pieces. 23 of them make your picture. One doesn't — it belongs to someone else in the room.
  2. Drag or tap two pieces to swap them. Build your picture. The piece left over is outlined in red.
  3. You are also missing one piece. Someone else in the room is holding it.
  4. Get up. Look at other people's screens. The stray piece you're carrying belongs to whoever's picture it matches.
  5. To hand a piece over: you press give, they press take, they tap the three symbols showing on your screen. Then you hold the red cross to release it.
  6. You cannot take a trade back. If you give a piece to the wrong person, you've broken their board, not yours.
  7. When every board in the room is whole, look up.

Playing alone? The room tab shows all the other boards, and you trade from there.
